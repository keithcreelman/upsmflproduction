Expired Rookie Auction (ERA) — players whose rookie contract expired (contract_type='Rookie' AND contractYear=0) and were not extended by the rookie extension deadline (Thu before Memorial Day weekend). Starting bid $1K, 36-hour lock window, $1K bid increments. Each owner may submit 1 nomination per 12-hour window (windows anchored to 6 AM / 6 PM ET). Opens Memorial Day Mon 6 AM ET; nominations close at the end of the Wed 6 PM → Thu 6 AM ET window (6 windows total). Forced retention through FA Auction close.
