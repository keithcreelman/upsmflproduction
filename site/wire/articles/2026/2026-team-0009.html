C-Town Chivalry Let Two Big Salaries Go and Bought Better Ones

Bear Dunn released George Pickens and Tony Pollard on the day the auction roster locked, then spent $152,000 on Christian McCaffrey, Joe Burrow and more. The preseason simulation ranks C-Town 1st, with the league's best title odds at 20.8%.

Early Preseason

Dunn started the offseason with one of the two best rosters in the league. In April he had 17 active players, not enough to field a lineup, $215,000 in salary, and 5 players who rank in the top twelve at their position: Jayden Daniels, James Cook, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, a High-end WR1, Drake London and Brock Bowers, an Elite TE1. Breece Hall and Tee Higgins were solid behind them. The weak spots were Tony Pollard, a Low-end RB3, and no second quarterback for the superflex.

Before the draft he kept it simple. LaPorta's rookie deal was running out, so in May Dunn extended him for 2 years at a TCV of $50,000, back-loaded: $10,000 this year and $40,000 in 2027. The other three expiring rookies, Hendon Hooker, Jake Haener and Jonathan Mingo, were let go. He cut Dyami Brown, whose $1,000 salary cost a $1,000 penalty, and released Tyler Higbee for nothing. No trades.

Pre-Auction

The tag came first. In May Dunn tagged George Pickens at $51,000 and kept his options open until lock day. The draft was an afterthought, 2 late picks, both defenders, Caleb Downs at 4.09 and Jake Golday at 6.09, and he bought Brian Branch at the Expired Rookie Auction for $4,000.

The week before the lock, everything moved. He extended James Cook by one year, adding $37,000 for 2027 on top of his $27,000 this year, $64,000 over the two seasons. In the three-way deal with Real Deal Creel and Blake Bombers he sent Drake London and $19,000 of blind-bid money away for Tetairoa McMillan, shedding $52,000 of salary and taking on $7,000; the trade bot graded his side a C+. And on lock day he cashed the chip: Pickens was released with no penalty, all $51,000 straight back to him, and Tony Pollard went the same day, $27,000 of salary off the books for a $13,500 penalty.

That is why the roster shrank before the auction, from 17 active players in April to 15 at the lock, and the salary from $215,000 to $137,000. Brown, Higbee, Pollard and London went out, McMillan and Branch came in, and Pickens came and went. None of it was an accident: it was room for the auction.

The Auction and Preseason Contracts

C-Town went into the Free Agent Auction with room it had made on purpose. James Cook's new deal was restructured the same day it was signed, down to $14,000 this year and up to $50,000 in 2027 with the $64,000 total untouched. Then he spent: $152,000 on 19 players, Christian McCaffrey for $63,000 after seventeen bids, Joe Burrow for $58,000 and DK Metcalf for $11,000.

McCaffrey came with a history. Sex Manther had carried him on a tag since before April and released him in July, and Dunn paid barely more than the tag.

What it bought: McCaffrey, a High-end RB1, and Burrow, a High-end QB1, the two Very good buys, and Metcalf, who starts. No owner's offense improved more at the auction, though CBP's improved by almost exactly as much; C-Town tops the league table by a hair. Burrow signed for 2 years, back-loaded, a TCV of $116,000: $28,000 this year and $88,000 in 2027, one of 6 multi-year contracts from the auction. After it he trimmed only depth, releasing Patrick Queen and three depth players at no cost.

The lineup it built has no holes: 6 top-twelve starters in Burrow, McCaffrey, Cook, Smith-Njigba, Bowers and Daniels in the superflex, with Higgins, Hall and McMillan solid around them. His backfield ranks 1st in the league, his quarterbacks 3rd, his receivers 3rd and his tight ends 3rd. On MFL's projections, two of his cheapest buys start on defense: Chase Young and Blake Cashman grade a High-end DL1 and a High-end LB1 and cost $1,000 and $2,000. The kicker, Jake Bates, is a High-end PK1.

The injury that matters is at tight end. Bowers is listed out for the opener with a meniscus injury, and LaPorta, extended in May, is next up. Tua Tagovailoa is questionable on the bench, and Branch, the Expired Rookie Auction buy, is on injured reserve into October, so the forecast leaves him out of the first four weeks.

History and the Division

Dunn has been here since 2011, and his record is heavy on everything but titles. His teams have had the league's best all-play record 4 times, in 2016, 2019, 2022, 2024, with 6 division titles and 9 playoff trips, but one championship, in 2016. His career all-play record is 56.7%.

His last two division titles came in the old alignment. This is year one of a new one, chosen in the league's first Owner Divisional Draft, and Dunn built DOG POUND 4 LIFE himself. He sat 3rd among the four captains, took the Hawks 3rd overall, exactly as he had announced in January, and came back for Cleon Ca$h 6th, the team he had called inseparable from his own in January. The simulation makes it his division to lose: 57.2% for C-Town, 24.4% for Cleon Ca$h and 18.4% for the Hawks, with C-Town at 74.9% to make the playoffs and 36.2% to earn a bye.

The division weeks are 1, 4, 8, 11 and 14, and nobody has a bye in the first two. The trouble starts in week eight, when McCaffrey and Chase Young are off, but so are four Cleon Ca$h starters, Malik Nabers and Jaxson Dart among them. Week eleven is the hard one: Smith-Njigba and three of his defenders sit, against Bijan Robinson and Tucker Kraft for Cleon Ca$h and Matthew Stafford and Kyren Williams for the Hawks. The last one, week fourteen, costs C-Town only Budda Baker and costs Cleon Ca$h Jeremiyah Love and CeeDee Lamb. Across the five weeks he loses 7 starters to byes, Cleon Ca$h 9 and the Hawks 9.

The next three years, told the way Dunn would tell them. 2027: C-Town won the whole thing. It had been eleven years. He posted one line about it and logged off. 2028: He won the division again, and he had built the roster without saying much at all. 2029: Cleon Ca$h won the title, out of his own division.

Week one is a division week. With 4 NFL teams done, Smith-Njigba had a long touchdown and a big day, McCaffrey chipped in, and C-Town has 42.4 points in the bank.
