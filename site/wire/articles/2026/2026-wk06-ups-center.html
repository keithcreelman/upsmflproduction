Has Bear been dominant since Week 1? Almost. Week 1 was the one miss: 190.3, 9th in the league and 3–8 against the field. Since then it is 53–2 over Weeks 2 to 6, and never lower than second in scoring. Week 6 itself was 267.3, the best score of the week, a 3–0 week and 11–0 against the field.

Stuart

And the stretch everyone is going to talk about?

Rich

That is a separate measurement, a streak and not the season. Over the last four weeks, Weeks 3 to 6, Bear is 43–1 against the field, with a clean 11–0 in Weeks 3, 5 and 6.

Rich

Stuart

Then what is left?

Rich

The bye. The top two seeds skip the first round, and Bear is at 93.6% to take one. The other one is open: Eric Martel 48.5%, Brian Cutting 30.8%, Shawn Blake 20.8%. Nobody else is above 3.4%.

Kenny

In Week 6, Bear’s biggest margin, 116.4, came against Josh Martel. Josh scored 150.9, the lowest score of the week, and also made 41 of the 163 owner posts in the chat since last Thursday night, more than any other owner. Rich says correlation is not our department.

Stuart

So one bye is spoken for, three teams are chasing the other, and the middle of the league is getting squeezed.

3 Playoff Picture — updated after Week 6
Stuart

10,000 simulated seasons, run fresh through Week 6. Rich, start with the part nobody is arguing about.

Rich

Bear: 99.8% to make the playoffs, 93.6% for a bye and 32.5% for the title, the best title odds in the league. With the Dog Pound at 97.8% for Bear, Klingenberg (44.1%) and Mannila (9.6%) are playing for a wild card, and wild cards go by All-Play. That is why Mannila, who is 9–7 with the worst All-Play of the four, is at 9.6% while Klingenberg, same record, is at 44.1%.

Stuart

Legion of Boom is the strange one. Derrick Whitman is 10–6, a game ahead of Shawn Blake, and the forecast gives Blake the division, 66.6% to 32.7%.

Rich

The forecast plays each team’s best lineup every week. Built from 2026 points per game with everyone healthy, Blake’s best lineup is 277.6 a week, second only to Bear’s 291.6. Whitman’s is 243.2, fifth. Whitman has the record and records count in a division race, so he keeps 32.7%. But the 0–3 week was more a lineup problem than a talent problem, and the forecast is betting on talent.

Stuart

Rich

Eric Martel 62.3%, Brian Cutting 37.6%. Martel is 11–5 and Cutting is 8–8, but Cutting’s best healthy lineup, 273.1 a week, is third in the league, and the forecast has Cutting at 97.4% to make the playoffs and 27.5% to win the title, second only to Bear. Last week the forecast said good roster, bad Sundays. It still does.

Keith Creelman?
