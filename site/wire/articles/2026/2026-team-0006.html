Early Preseason

April found The Long Haulers top-heavy. Cross carried 20 active players and $270,000 in salary, with 5 real starters on offense: Jalen Hurts, a High-end QB1, Derrick Henry at running back, A.J. Brown at receiver, Tyler Warren, a High-end TE1, and Trevor Lawrence in the superflex. The 4 holes were Jordan Mason at the second running back spot, Josh Downs at the second receiver spot, and Tre Tucker and Woody Marks in the two flexes, all replacement-level.

He did his shopping before the draft, and paid in picks, not salary. The trade bot was not grading deals yet; graded now on what was known on each trade's date, the three spring trades come out C, C and B. In April his 2027 first- and second-round picks went to HammerTime for Hurts, in the last year of a deal HammerTime had restructured the season before, and HammerTime added $20,000 of blind-bid money, so Cross was paid to take on a $67,000 quarterback. In May a 2027 third bought Quentin Johnston from Pure Greatness, and five later-round rookie picks went to Sex Manther for 3.04 and 3.06.

Trevor Lawrence was already on a $37,000 tag by April, and there were no cuts and no extensions in that stretch. Two rookie deals ran out: Devon Witherspoon is still on the roster, and Will Levis went to the Expired Rookie Auction, where Cross bought him back.

Pre-Auction

On draft night he kept 3.04 and used it on Carson Beck, and flipped 3.06 with 2.03 to Gride for 2.05 and 2.10, a B from the bot; Gride sent 3.06 on to Real Deal Creel an hour later. He made 7 picks: Fernando Mendoza at 1.03, a slot that has produced a usable player more often than not, then Malachi Fields, Germie Bernard, Mike Washington Jr., Beck, Kevin Coleman and Keldric Faulk. Mendoza and Beck make two rookie quarterbacks behind Hurts and Lawrence. None of the class grades as a starter yet, which at this stage is the expectation, not a verdict.

At the Expired Rookie Auction he bought Tyjae Spears for $1,000 and Levis back for $1,000. His cuts before the lock were small: six fringe players at no cost, and one real release, Theo Johnson in July, and his $5,000 salary. A July trade with L.A. Looks sent Isaiah Bond and a 2027 fourth for safety Jordan Battle, Adonai Mitchell and a 2027 fifth. The bot first graded it a D+ for him, then posted a correction the next morning: it had run the trade backwards, and his side was an A, the winner. And the night before the auction roster locked he extended Hurts one more year, $52,000 for 2027, a TCV of $119,000 over two seasons.

The Auction and Preseason Contracts

The Long Haulers spent $39,000 across the two auctions, and 71.8% of it went on defenders: Maxx Crosby, an Elite DL1, for $8,000, Jordyn Brooks, a High-end LB1, for $4,000, and Brian Burns for $7,000. It is a real defense, and like every defense in this league it is a footnote to the offense.

On offense he spent $7,000 on three players: George Holani, who starts at the second running back spot, and Jalen Nailor and Nick Mullens, who do not. That is a net-neutral auction for the lineup, and the league table, which ranks every owner by how much the auction improved his starting offense, puts The Long Haulers 10th of 12, with only Pure Greatness and Cleon Ca$h below him.

The contract work ran through the auction. The morning of the lock Cross restructured Hurts, back-loading him from $67,000 to $47,000 this year and $72,000 in 2027 with the $119,000 total unchanged, room he had in hand as the bidding opened. It is the second time Hurts has been back-loaded in two years: HammerTime did it in 2025, and Cross did it the morning after extending him. 6 auction buys and both Expired Rookie Auction buys got multi-year contracts, and so did two trade pickups, Battle and Gunnar Helm. One of those did not last: Levis, bought back and signed on, was cut in August with a $1,000 penalty that lands on the 2027 cap. Josh Downs was extended one more year in September, $22,000 for 2027.

After the auction came the churn: 9 pickups before week one, three of them tight ends passing through. Zach Ertz, Darren Waller and Matthew Hibner all came and went, Helm arrived from Sex Manther in a trade the bot graded A, and Najee Harris and Malik Davis joined at running back. The 13 releases in that stretch cost $3,000 in penalties, all of it charged to 2027.

His lineup today has the shape it had in April: 5 real starters in Hurts, Henry, Brown, Warren and Lawrence, and 4 holes, now Holani at the second running back spot, Johnston at the second receiver spot, and Mason and Downs in the flexes. The kicker, Jason Myers, led all kickers in points per game last year and cost $1,000, and the punter, Jeremy Crawshaw, grades a Mid PN1.

The injury that matters is Brown's. He caught three passes in the opener and left in the third quarter with a right high-ankle sprain; an MRI confirmed it, and NFL Network reports he will miss at least four weeks. MFL has not listed him yet, so the forecast holds him out of weeks two through four by hand, and a receiver room that was already the thin part of the roster gets thinner.

History and the Division

Cross is a short story so far. He took over the franchise late in 2024, and the league neither credits nor blames him for that season, so his record starts in 2025: one completed season, a 39.0% all-play record and no playoff trip. This is year two, and it is the easiest kind of history to improve on.

He hosted the league's first Owner Divisional Draft at his house and then went first in it, to Eric Martel's HammerTime, which by the room's own rule of thumb meant his captain thought he was the weakest team in the draft. Cross leaned all the way in: when Ryan Bousquet said it was going to be a long haul, Cross allowed that at least he had picked the right team name, and a month later he pitched names for the division, the best of them casting The Long Haulers as the problem next to two recent champions. The simulation puts Outlast PG Time in the champions' hands, Pure Greatness at 47.0% and HammerTime at 34.5%, and The Long Haulers at 18.5% with 38.0% playoff odds.

The division weeks are 1, 4, 8, 11 and 14, and the byes cut both ways. Week eight costs him Burns and Tremaine Edmunds, while HammerTime loses Stroud, its only startable quarterback. Week eleven is his worst, four starters at once, Holani, Brown, Myers and Witherspoon. And week fourteen, the last of the regular season, is the only division week in which he loses nobody while both rivals lose starters. Across the five weeks he loses 6 starters to byes, HammerTime 8 and Pure Greatness 6.

The next three years, filed the way Cross would file them. 2027: no first-round pick, because HammerTime owns it, and he congratulates HammerTime by name when they use it. Crosby, Burns and Brooks are still under contract. 2028: Mendoza or Beck takes the superflex from Lawrence, the team finally fills the second receiver spot, and it is Let's GO season in the group chat. 2029, and this is a prediction, not a verdict: a winning record, and a promise about 2030.

Week one is a division week, and it has already cost him. With 4 NFL teams done, Myers kicked two field goals and Brown caught three passes before the ankle, and The Long Haulers have 17.1 points in the bank.
