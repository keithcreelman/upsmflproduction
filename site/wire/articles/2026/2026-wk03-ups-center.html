How this was built — 5 notes
Sources. MFL scores, rosters, schedules and injury tags; the league's archived Discord chat; a season simulation run through week 3's results for the Week 4 preview; expected fantasy points from nflverse ffopportunity for the bust/bargain grading.
Format. This is the pilot of Boomer's Three-Minute Rundown, a guest-host segment tossed to and from inside the regular Stuart-and-Rich desk -- an on-page UPS Center bit, not an actual ESPN appearance or contribution.
One current forecast. League Landscape's "Current PO" and the Week 4 matchup odds both come from the same model, re-run through this week's own results (entering week 4). The Preseason column is fixed at draft day and never updates. A separate preseason-forecast simulation exists in the pack but was last refreshed through week 2 -- it is not used for any "current" claim on this page.
What this preview does not claim. Preseason-vs-now movement is shown as one combined change, not split into a result effect and a strength effect -- that split needs a banked-only re-simulation on the original preseason inputs, which are no longer available.
