Early Preseason

April was a teardown. Creelman carried 14 active players, not enough for a lineup, and just $62,000 in salary, the smallest commitment in the league. Dallas Goedert was the one top-twelve player and Tetairoa McMillan was solid; the rest, Deshaun Watson, TreVeyon Henderson, Christian Watson, Marvin Harrison Jr., Trey Benson and Keon Coleman, were stopgaps, and Emanuel Wilson's rookie deal was running out.

The first big move came in April, and it was an extend-and-trade: Kenneth Walker, extended a year and then shipped, the largest contract he moved all year at $32,000, went to HammerTime for their 2027 first. The trade bot was not grading deals yet; graded now as of that day, it comes out A for Real Deal Creel. Blake Watson was released from the taxi squad at no cost. Two rookie deals ran out: Emanuel Wilson went to Blake Bombers at the Expired Rookie Auction and Jalen Carter was let go.

Pre-Auction

Draft night he made three trades, and the bot graded all three as middling: 1.04 to Gride for 1.07 and Travis Etienne, whom Gride had tagged three days earlier, a C; 2.09 and a 2027 third to Pure Greatness for 2.06, a C+; and 3.09 and 3.12 to Gride for 3.06, a C. That last pick had been CBP's, then L.A. Looks', CBP's again, Sex Manther's and The Long Haulers', and Gride had held it for an hour; Creelman used it less than a minute after the trade, on Sonny Styles, who already projects as a Mid LB1. He made 5 picks in all: KC Concepcion at 1.07, a slot that has produced a usable player more often than not, Omar Cooper Jr. at 1.09, De'Zhaun Stribling, Styles and Zion Young. He was the busiest buyer at the Expired Rookie Auction too, 5 players for $9,000: Chris Rodriguez, Kayshon Boutte, Michael Mayer, Kendre Miller and Will McDonald.

In July Creelman made room. He cut Noah Fant ($4,000 of salary, a $1,000 penalty), Trey Benson ($6,000, $1,500) and Keon Coleman ($5,000, $1,250), and in the three-way with C-Town and Blake Bombers he sent McMillan, his own 2027 first, Rodriguez and Omar Cooper for Puka Nacua, an Elite WR1, moving $17,000 of salary out and $22,000 in, an A- from the bot. The first-round pick he gave up was his own; the one Walker bought, HammerTime's, is still his. He went into the auction with 16 active players and $95,000 in active-roster salary.

The Auction and Preseason Contracts

Real Deal Creel spent $189,000 at the Free Agent Auction, more than anyone, on 19 players: Dak Prescott for $63,000 after fifteen bids, Saquon Barkley for $45,000, Kyler Murray for $32,000, and Jacoby Brissett, Blake's July cut, for $13,000 as insurance. Hours after winning Prescott, in the middle of the auction, he made more room by back-loading Nacua's deal to $9,000 this year and $35,000 in 2027, freeing $13,000; he put it back as it was 40 days later, four days before the season opened, and the total never changed.

The auction bought Prescott and Barkley, the two Very good buys; Murray, the Good one in the superflex; and Parker Washington, who starts. The league table ranks Real Deal Creel 3rd of 12 for how much the auction improved the offense.

The churn came after the auction: 14 waiver pickups before week one, more than anyone, most of them one-for-one swaps at the back of the roster, and Trey Benson among them, claimed back less than three weeks after he was cut. Most of the multi-year contracts came in September. The two big ones were front-loaded: Washington for 3 years at a TCV of $15,000, $12,000 this year, and Chig Okonkwo for 2 at $14,000, $11,000 this year. The rest were small deals: Brian Robinson, Stefon Diggs and, back in July, Cam Little from the auction, the two Expired Rookie Auction buys and 4 waiver pickups. He traded George Karlaftis and a 2027 fifth to the Hawks for their fourth, an A from the bot, and cut Deshaun Watson with a $1,000 penalty charged to 2027. He sits at $3,000 of room.

The lineup it built has 4 top-twelve starters, Prescott, Barkley, Nacua and Goedert, with Etienne and Murray solid, and 3 holes: Christian Watson at receiver and Washington and Henderson in the flexes. The defense is the soft spot, which is a little awkward for the owner who has added 428 defenders since 2010, the most in the league: on MFL's projections only Nick Bolton among the starters grades above replacement level, and Sonny Styles, the rookie who already projects as a Mid LB1, is on the taxi squad.

Henderson is out for the opener with an ankle, so the forecast sits him in week one and Marvin Harrison Jr. is next up in the flex. James Pearce is suspended into November, and Benson, cut and claimed back, is on injured reserve for the season.

History and the Division

Creelman has been here since 2010, all 16 completed seasons, and the record has one gap that everyone in the league knows about: 9 playoff trips, 2 division titles, runner-up finishes in 2013 and 2019, and no title. His career all-play record is 53.1%.

He started the Owner Divisional Draft talk in January and then was drafted himself: Josh Martel, the last of the four captains, took Real Deal Creel 4th overall and L.A.
