Gride Traded for St. Brown and Bid for Mahomes

Matt Gerardi made 5 trades between the draft and the lock, flipped a tag into Jadarian Price and a same-day extension into Amon-Ra St. Brown, and paid $48,000 for Patrick Mahomes. The preseason simulation still ranks Gride 12th, and there is a lot of season to change that.

Early Preseason

In April Gerardi had 13 active players, not enough for a lineup, and $154,000 in salary. None of them ranked in the top twelve at his position; Sam Darnold and Cam Skattebo were solid, and the rest, from DJ Moore, Deebo Samuel and Michael Pittman to David Njoku and Bryce Young, were stopgaps.

Before the draft, the big move was Bryce Young. His rookie deal was running out, and in May Gerardi extended him for $33,000 a year for 2 years, a TCV of $66,000. The other three expiring rookies, Rodriguez, Kendre Miller and Tyjae Spears, went to the Expired Rookie Auction and to other teams.

Pre-Auction

Gerardi tagged Travis Etienne at $19,000 in May, and three days later traded him: on draft night Etienne and 1.07 went to Real Deal Creel for 1.04, a B- from the trade bot, and Gride took Jadarian Price with it. He made three more deals that night: 2.05 and 2.10 to The Long Haulers for 2.03 and 3.06, a B; a 2027 second to Pure Greatness for 2.09 and 4.12, a B+; and, about an hour after getting it, 3.06 to Real Deal Creel for 3.09 and 3.12, which the bot graded A. He came out with 10 picks, three of them first-rounders: Price at 1.04, Jordyn Tyson at 1.05, a slot that has produced a usable player half the time, and Antonio Williams at 1.11, plus Chris Bell at 2.03 and Trey Smack at 6.03, who is already his starting kicker.

In July Gerardi extended Sam Darnold one more year, $28,000 for 2027, and the same day sent him to L.A. Looks with Coker and Emmett Johnson for Amon-Ra St. Brown, Shedeur Sanders and $10,000 of blind-bid money, a B+ from the bot. That deal alone took on $53,000 of salary and sent $24,000 away, and it bought a High-end WR1. On lock day he cut Pittman and his $45,000 salary to make the room back.

The Auction and Preseason Contracts

Gride spent $101,000 at the Free Agent Auction, almost all of it on two quarterbacks. Patrick Mahomes cost $48,000 and Malik Willis $39,000 after fourteen bids; every other one of his 16 wins cost the minimum. Mahomes is the Very good buy and Willis the Good one, and the league table ranks Gride 8th of 12 for how much the auction improved the offense.

Mahomes signed for 3 years at a TCV of $144,000 the day he was won and was restructured the same day, with the total unchanged. Willis got his multi-year deal in September, 2 years at $39,000 a year. No waiver claims, one auction receiver let go before week one, and $22,000 of room left.

The lineup it built has 2 top-twelve starters, Mahomes and St. Brown, with Skattebo and Willis solid, and 5 holes: Price at the second running back spot, DJ Moore at receiver, Hunter Henry at tight end, and Deebo Samuel and Kimani Vidal in the flexes. His backs and his tight ends both rank last in the league. Graded on MFL's projections, only Myles Murphy and Kamari Lassiter on the defense start above replacement level. Bryce Young, extended in May, is on the bench.

No starter is hurt, but the rookie class is. Tyson, the 1.05 pick, is on injured reserve into October, and Chris Brazzell II is out for the season. Alex Wright, a veteran defender bought at the auction for the minimum, is also out for the year with an Achilles.

History and the Division

Gerardi has been here since 2016, 10 completed seasons, and his one title, in 2022, came in one of his two division-winning years, 2019 and 2022. It is a thin record around a real trophy: 3 playoff trips and a 41.4% career all-play record.

Derrick Whitman took Gride 2nd overall in the league's first Owner Divisional Draft, the first pick of his captaincy, and LEGION OF BOOM is a hard room for the team the simulation ranks last. Blake Bombers are 3rd and the division favorite at 52.2%, CBP is at 36.0%, and Gride is at 11.8%, with 20.9% playoff odds.

The division weeks are 1, 4, 8, 11 and 14, and the byes fall about evenly. Week eight costs him Skattebo, Samuel and Kamari Lassiter, three starters, the same as CBP and Blake Bombers lose. Week eleven takes Price, Henry, Smack and Divine Deablo, while CBP loses six. Week fourteen, the last of the regular season, has no byes anywhere in the division. Across the five weeks he loses 7 starters to byes, CBP 9 and Blake Bombers 7.

The next three years: in 2027 the rookies arrive, Price and Tyson start, and Gride misses the playoffs by a game. In 2028, the last year of Mahomes's deal, the phones stay busy. In 2029 Gride makes the playoffs.

Week one is a division week. With 4 NFL teams done, Deebo Samuel has scored a touchdown and Gride has 36.2 points in the bank.
