1 The Big Story — Eric Martel set the league’s single-week scoring record
Stuart

Eric Martel scored 328.0 in Week 9, the highest single-week score in league history (every week with all twelve teams scored, 2011–2026 so far, playoffs included: 2,712 team-weeks). The previous record was 325.5 by Steve Bousquet in Week 11 of 2021.

Rich

Scoring has risen across the eras, so here is the other measure. 328.0 was 158.7% of that week’s league average of 206.7. Only three team-weeks since 2011 have posted a bigger share: Bear Dunn in 2017 (176.4%, the best share ever), Steve Bousquet in 2021 (168.5%), and Ryan Bousquet in 2014 (Week 15, a playoff week, 160.4%).

Kenny

Bear Dunn already had the old record in hand, 325.5 by Steve Bousquet in Week 11 of 2021. Sunday at 8:51 PM: “325.5 by Steve.”, and then, Sunday at 8:52 PM: “Your team firing big time today”.

Kenny

Eric Martel had his own read Sunday at 8:53 PM: “With no jacobs or ladd... crazy week”. The week was 328.0, 158.7% of that week’s league average, and Eric Martel posted 12 times.

Stuart

Eric Martel won all three games: Chris Klingenberg by 150.6, Eric Mannila by 118.7, and Bear Dunn by 54.1.

Stuart

Sunday was the record. The other story of the week arrived Tuesday, and it was big: Keith Creelman and Eric Mannila made a nine-asset trade at 2:27 PM ET. Creelman sent Christian Watson (WR), KC Concepcion (WR), Saquon Barkley (RB), Kyler Murray (QB), Eric Martel’s 2027 first-round pick, and Keith Creelman’s 2027 second-round pick. Mannila sent Bijan Robinson (RB), CeeDee Lamb (WR), and Kirk Cousins (QB).

Rich

Look at where Creelman started. Creelman’s running backs scored 151.2 points through Week 9, dead last among the twelve teams, so the headline coming back is Bijan Robinson, a running back.

Kenny

Keith Creelman had put the need in the chat Monday at 5:16 PM: “I’m fine at WR…but I’ll take an RB 😂”. The next afternoon Bijan Robinson was on Creelman’s roster.

Stuart

Christian Watson is the piece that tells the other half of the story. The cheapest player in the deal, at $1,000, and started all nine weeks for Creelman. Watson went to Mannila as part of the package for Bijan Robinson, CeeDee Lamb, and Kirk Cousins.

Kenny

Josh Martel weighed in Tuesday at 2:31 PM on what Mannila took in: “Conception & Watson are both degrees of very nice. Plus the draft capital. I see the reasoning”.

Rich

The players Creelman sent carry $87,000 in salary, and the players Mannila sent carry $83,000. Only 38 of the 1,036 trades in league history since 2010 moved nine assets or more.

Kenny

Shawn Blake had the league bot on his mind that afternoon. Tuesday at 2:30 PM: “I just got email. Let’s see if bot roasts it this time”, and then, still waiting on it: “The bot can’t is still processing the information.” and “Can’t compute”.

Kenny

Ryan Bousquet wanted a ruling on Sunday (7:01 PM): “Did Cousins have the most negative score in a play in UPS history?”. Cousins had a quiet one: 13.0 points on Mannila’s bench. But Ryan asked the right question about the wrong player. The lowest weekly score any player has ever posted in UPS history is −14.6, and it belongs to Mark Sanchez, Week 4 of 2011, the Jets against the Ravens: 11-of-35 for 119 yards, no touchdowns, one interception, two sacks, and three fumbles lost. Sanchez was on Mannila’s bench for it, so it never counted: that team scored 131.9.

Kenny

The second lowest is −11.0, and this one was started by Mannila: Nathan Peterman, Week 11 of 2017, the Bills against the Chargers: 6-of-14 for 66 yards, no touchdowns, and five interceptions. That week Mannila’s team scored 92.3, the lowest in the league by 45.8 points. Two lowest weeks in league history, one benched and one started, both Mannila’s.

Kenny

Then, Tuesday at 3:28 PM, as Cousins changed rosters, Ryan Bousquet asked again: “Anyone remember who was on the other side of that Cousins outburst?”. The answer, for the record: on the other side of Cousins that week was Eric Martel, whose 328.0 beat Mannila by 118.7.
