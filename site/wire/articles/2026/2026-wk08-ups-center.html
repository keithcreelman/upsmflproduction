1 The Big Story — Bear Dunn is on a historic pace
Stuart

Bear Dunn scored 307.6 in Week 8, the best score of the week, and beat all eleven teams in All-Play. He swept both games: Eric Mannila by 89.5 and Chris Klingenberg by 78.4. He is 19–2 overall and 78–10 in All-Play.

Rich

Here is what 78–10 means. Since 2011, 180 teams have reached Week 8 with a full record in the league archive. Exactly one had a better All-Play record at this point: Josh Martel’s 2018 team, which was 84–4.

Stuart

That is .886 for Bear. Martel’s 2018 start was .955. After Bear, the next-best starts are Derrick Whitman’s 2013 team at 73–15 and his 2015 team at 72–16, then Martel again in 2021 at 70–18.

2 Division Week — four triangles, four sweeps, one playoff race
Stuart

Every game in Week 8 was played inside a division: four triangles of three teams, twelve games in all, and the same script in each one. One team swept. One split. One went home with nothing.

Rich

Dog Pound. Bear Dunn is the sweep, and you know that part. The pain is on the other side. Eric Mannila got 38.5 from Tyler Allgeier and 36.3 from Kirk Cousins and went 0–2 anyway. He lost to Chris Klingenberg by 11.1, and his best possible lineup, 244.5, would have won that game. Klingenberg split, and he is 13–8 overall but 1–5 against his own division. Everything he has won, he has won somewhere else.
