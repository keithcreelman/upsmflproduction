Week one, from the desk

The Champ Won on Offense. Eric Martel Won the Game. Boo-yah.

The forecast's favorite scored ninth, the man who paid the auction's top price for Josh Allen did not win a game, and the guy who runs this website swept his division while starting a quarterback who went backwards.

The Lead

Stuart: Welcome to the Wire, everybody. Week one is in the books, and before anybody gets comfortable I have to drop some knowledge. The preseason forecast told you Bear Dunn was No. 1. Then the games started, and Bear Dunn posted the 9th-most points in the league. Rich?

Rich: Thanks, Stu. Meanwhile the man ranked No. 4, the team the preview called stacked everywhere but quarterback, went 11-0 against the field. Eric Martel, 312.8, the most points in the league. He GOT it.

Stuart: And his best receiver barely showed up! Ja'Marr Chase scored 3.4 on a projection of 23.3. Did not matter one bit. Kenneth Walker III scored 38.5 on a projection of 8.6. Cool as the other side of the pillow.

Rich: But look where the rest of it came from, Stu. 100.3 points from his defenders, the most IDP points in the league. T.J. Watt alone: 4 tackles, 2 sacks, 1 INT, 1 TD, good for 29.0. Did you know? Brian Cutting, the defending champion, put up the most offensive points in the league and still lost to him.

Stuart: The champ won the offense and lost the game, and Martel's defenders more than doubled his. Quentin Lake chipped in 20.0 from the secondary. See, what had happened was, the other team brought defenders. Rich!

Rich: Let's check the rest of the forecast's homework. Bear Dunn came in with 18.1% title odds, the best in the league. He went 3-8 against the field. And the best pickup of the week, Mike Gesicki (22.3), was Bear Dunn's. He scored every one of those points on Bear's bench.

Stuart: Now, the projections backed his lineup calls, so this is not a bad call, this is a bad Sunday. He still left 38.7 points on that bench, and he had a winning lineup on his bench. Rich, you have to do the next one. I cannot.

Rich: In the interest of full disclosure, Stu: the man who runs this website. Keith Creelman spent more than anyone at the auction, and one of the things he bought was Kyler Murray. Week one: Kyler Murray scored -0.2 on a projection of 13.1.

Stuart: Negative! The man went backwards! And on the bench, the insurance policy he bought at that same auction. Jacoby Brissett (22.1) sat while Kyler Murray started for -0.2. The projections going in: Jacoby Brissett projected 15.3, Kyler Murray projected 13.1. That is not bad luck, that is a bad call. Can I get a witness from the congregation?

Rich: Witness here, and the congregation says guilty. He won both games anyway, and against Josh Martel he can thank a receiver: Christian Watson scored 36.7 on a projection of 15.3. The site owner leaned on Christian Watson, and yes, Keith, it is in the recap. It is in the recap right now.

Stuart: Now the best line in the league. Caleb Williams: 21-of-29 for 269, 2 TD; 10 carries for 65 and 2 TD. 43.9 points for Josh Martel. He must be the bus driver, because he was taking the Carolina defense to school.

Rich: And the biggest surprise belonged to a man who lost twice. Jalen Coker scored 38.0 on a projection of 7.8, for Ryan Bousquet, who got Coker from Matt Gerardi in the Amon-Ra St. Brown trade along with Sam Darnold. Sam Darnold scored 0.2 on a projection of 13.7. Same trade, Stu. Let's go to the pots.

The Pots

Stuart: Four divisions, four pots, every game a division game. In every pot, somebody went two-and-oh and somebody went oh-and-two. The pot of the week is Outlast PG Time, picked as the strongest division, and it delivered the most points of the four pots. Rich?

Rich: And here is the stat of the week, Stu. Of the four preseason division favorites, exactly one won both of his games. He runs this website. The other three went a combined two-and-four.

The Landscape

Stuart: Four divisions, four leaders, every one of them two-and-oh: Eric Martel (2-0), Eric Mannila (2-0), Keith Creelman (2-0) and Derrick Whitman (2-0). Rich, check the forecast.

Rich: The top three in the preseason rankings were Bear Dunn, Brian Cutting and Shawn Blake. None of them won both games. Blake did not win one. The owners ranked fourth, fifth, seventh and eighth all went two-and-oh.

Stuart: And the bottom of the board? No. 12 Matt Gerardi posted the 5th-most points in the league. The forecast gave him 1.7% to win the whole thing, and he just beat Shawn Blake. Better recognize.

Rich: Easy, Stu. It is one week of a long season, and one week is a data point, not a verdict. Bear Dunn had a winning lineup on his bench, and nobody in this league should be writing him off after one Sunday.

Stuart: Fair. But Eric Martel went 11-0 against the field. It's your world, kid. The rest of us are still paying rent. Rich, take us home.

Rich: That is the show. Chris Klingenberg, week two is a fresh card. Keith, bench the negative quarterback. For Stu, I'm Rich. See you after week two.
