The Hawks Had No Quarterback in April and Two Starters by August

Chris Klingenberg made no moves in the spring beyond the rookie draft, then spent $106,000 at the Free Agent Auction, $38,000 of it on Jared Goff. The preseason simulation has the Hawks 10th, in a division with the league's top-ranked team.

Early Preseason

April was bare. The Hawks carried 19 active players and $142,000 in salary, and there was no quarterback on the roster at all, so Jakobi Meyers, a receiver, sat in the superflex. Kyren Williams, a High-end RB2, was the only solid starter, and there was not one top-twelve player at any position. The other 8 spots were holes: nobody at quarterback, J.K. Dobbins at the second running back spot, Mike Evans and Michael Wilson at receiver, Dalton Kincaid at tight end, and RJ Harvey, Chimere Dike and Meyers in the flexes.

Before the rookie draft the Hawks made no moves at all: no trade, no cut, no extension. 4 rookie deals ran out and none was kept: Drew Sanders, Luke Schoonmaker and Nolan Smith simply left, and Jordan Battle went to L.A. Looks at the Expired Rookie Auction.

Pre-Auction

The draft was the whole pre-auction offseason, and it produced two real starters, Carnell Tate and CJ Allen, plus a replacement-level one in Mansoor Delane. The Hawks made 7 picks, two of them in the first round: Carnell Tate at 1.02, a slot that has produced a usable player half the time, and Makai Lemon at 1.06. Tate went straight into the lineup as a Low-end WR3. Kaelon Black, Ja'Kobi Lane and a quarterback, Drew Allar, followed, and CJ Allen is already starting: taken early in the fourth round, he projects as a High-end LB1 on MFL's numbers.

No Expired Rookie Auction buys, no tags, no cuts and no trades until the roster locked. Klingenberg went into the auction with 21 active players and $166,000 in active-roster salary, which left room to spend, and he had a lot to fill.

The Auction and Preseason Contracts

The Hawks spent $106,000 at the Free Agent Auction, and most of it went on the position they did not have. Jared Goff cost $38,000 after twelve bids, the most contested of his wins, and Matthew Stafford $33,000, with Rachaad White at $13,000, Rhamondre Stevenson at $9,000 and Travis Kelce at $6,000 behind them.

What it bought: Stafford, a Low-end QB1, and Kelce, a Low-end TE1, the two Very good buys; Goff, a High-end QB2 and the Good one, in a superflex league where most lineups carry two quarterbacks; and Stevenson, who starts. White is bench depth. The league table ranks the Hawks 6th of 12 for how much the auction improved the offense.

The biggest buys signed long: Goff for 3 years at $38,000 a year, a TCV of $114,000, Stafford at $66,000 over two, and White at $39,000. He made no restructures. After the auction he traded a 2027 fourth to Real Deal Creel for George Karlaftis and a 2027 fifth, a C from the trade bot, cut Marquise Brown with $8,000 of salary and a $2,000 penalty charged to 2027, and claimed a backup kicker, Cairo Santos, and a punter, Sam Martin, off waivers.

The lineup it built has 2 top-twelve starters, Stafford and Kelce, and 5 holes: Stevenson at the second running back spot, Mike Evans and Carnell Tate at receiver, and Michael Wilson and J.K. Dobbins in the flexes. Receiver is where the Hawks rank lowest, 12th of 12. On MFL's projections the defense has a few real starters: CJ Allen is a High-end LB1, and Fred Warner and Chop Robinson grade as a High-end LB2 and a High-end DL2. The kicker, Andres Borregales, is a High-end PK1.

No starter carries an injury designation. Jeremiah Owusu-Koramoah is on injured reserve for the season, and two taxi-squad players are banged up.

History and the Division

Klingenberg has been here since 2011, 15 completed seasons, with no title, one runner-up finish in 2017, 3 playoff trips, and division titles in 2013 and 2021. His career all-play record of 36.9% is the lowest in the league.

This is year one of the alignment the owners drafted themselves, and Bear Dunn, seated third among the captains, took the Hawks 3rd overall, exactly as he had announced in January, the day the draft was first floated. DOG POUND 4 LIFE belongs to C-Town on paper: 57.6% to win it, against 23.6% for Cleon Ca$h and 18.8% for the Hawks, whose playoff odds are 36.4%.

The division weeks are 1, 4, 8, 11 and 14, with no byes in the first two, and one of them ties for the worst week on any roster in the division. Week eight costs him Evans and Warner. Week eleven takes five Hawks starters at once, Stafford, Kyren Williams, Stevenson, Borregales and Marcus Jones, against four for C-Town and three for Cleon Ca$h. Week fourteen, the last of the regular season, takes Wilson and DeMarvion Overshown. Across the five weeks he loses 9 starters to byes, C-Town 7 and Cleon Ca$h 10.

The next three years, Klingenberg style. 2027: the Hawks win DOG POUND 4 LIFE?!?! Goff and Stafford carry it... no, wait, strike that, C-Town wins it, bro, but the Hawks make the playoffs. 2028: Tate is a lead receiver and Lemon is right behind him, and this is the year, dude, this is definitely the year. It isn't: the Hawks miss the playoffs. 2029: Cleon Ca$h wins the title, out of this very division.

Week one is a division week. With 4 NFL teams done, Mike Evans caught a touchdown, Kyren Williams ran one in, and the Hawks already have 75.2 points in the bank. Stafford threw an interception in a quiet first game.
