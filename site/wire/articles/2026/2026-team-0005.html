Early Preseason

April was deep. Martel carried 20 active players and $219,000 in salary, with 4 top-twelve starters, Ja'Marr Chase, an Elite WR1, Trey McBride, an Elite TE1, Kenneth Walker and T.J. Hockenson in the superflex, and 0 holes. C.J. Stroud, Javonte Williams, Ladd McConkey, Montgomery and Luther Burden were all solid. It is also a lineup whose best quarterback grades as a Low-end QB2.

The spring was a salary swap. In March Martel extended Stroud's rookie deal for one more year at $22,000. In April he sent Jalen Hurts and $20,000 of blind-bid money to the Long Haulers for their 2027 first and second, which is paying to move a $67,000 quarterback, then spent his own 2027 first on Walker and his own second on Montgomery. Before the draft $67,000 of salary went out and $27,000 came in. Of his other expiring rookies, Sean Tucker went to Sex Manther at the Expired Rookie Auction and Tyree Wilson was let go.

Pre-Auction

Martel tagged Javonte Williams in May at $30,000, and unlike the other tags this summer this one was simply a keep: Williams is his second running back and grades as a High-end RB2. On draft day he moved up twice, sending 4.01, 4.08, 5.01 and two 2027 picks away for 2.12 and 3.08, and made 4 picks, Skyler Bell and Demond Claiborne first. The only release before the lock cost something small: Troy Franklin, $5,000 of salary with a $1,250 penalty.

The Auction and Preseason Contracts

With no hole to fill, Martel spent $69,000 at the Free Agent Auction mostly on depth and defense. Josh Jacobs was the big buy at $30,000, and Aidan Hutchinson, Micah Parsons, Ernest Jones and T.J. Watt came cheaply behind him. Jacobs, a Low-end RB2, is the one Good buy in the What it bought column, and the league table ranks the money 10th of 12 for what each dollar added, which says less about the auction than about how little there was to add.

The contracts were long. Jacobs signed a back-loaded deal at $18,000 this year and $90,000 in all, and Hutchinson, Jones and Parsons got multi-year deals too. There were no restructures. After the auction he cut Jonnu Smith, whose $6,000 salary carried a $7,500 penalty charged to 2027, and claimed 4 players off waivers, among them Quentin Lake and Jalen Nailor.

The lineup it built still has 0 holes and 4 top-twelve starters, Chase, McBride, Walker and Hockenson, with the league's best tight ends and its second-best receivers. The hole that the count does not show is under center: Stroud is the only quarterback in it, a tight end plays the superflex, and his quarterbacks rank 12th of 12. The defense and the kicker are footnotes, and good ones.

Historical Context

Martel is one of the league's newer owners, here since 2022, and he has made the most of it: a title in 2024, a division title in 2025, 3 playoff trips in 4 seasons, and a 55.6% career all-play record.
