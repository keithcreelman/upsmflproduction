The other story of the week arrived Tuesday, and it was big: Eric Martel and Brian Cross made an eight-asset trade at 5:02 AM ET. Rich

Stuart

Cross had given Eric Martel his own 2027 first-round and second-round picks in April for Jalen Hurts. Tuesday’s trade brought both picks back.

Stuart
