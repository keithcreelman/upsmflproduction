Trade Review Queue

Commissioner-only. Read-only hold-and-inspect surface for staged two-team War Room trades — fresh compliance, each owner's consent and conditional-drop selections, cap-acknowledgment status, and the real execution-ledger state. There is no execute or drop action on this page.

🚨 A staged trade cannot be completed yet.

This queue can tell you today's compliance checks clear and show exactly what it would send to MFL (below, per trade) — but nothing here can actually send it, and completion is unavailable regardless of compliance. Conditional-drop selection has no executor, and there is no commissioner action anywhere that finishes a trade for real. Do not enable cutover expecting this queue to also let you finish what it holds.

Sequence is decided (Keith's ruling, 2026-09-29): required drops confirm FIRST, one at a time, before the trade is ever attempted (docs/LOADED_CONTRACT_DROP_EXECUTION_DESIGN.md §2.2/§2.4.3b) — never the reverse, and never left to per-deal judgment. This guarantees a franchise's roster never exceeds the 5-loaded-contract limit because of a deal this app handles. It does not make the trade safe: if every required drop confirms and the trade itself then fails, that is a real, largely irreversible loss — restoration is a manual, race-prone workaround (get the player back before anyone else claims him, then restore his exact contract), never a guarantee, and this queue must say so wherever it shows a deal's status once a real completion action exists.
