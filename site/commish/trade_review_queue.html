Trade Review Queue

Commissioner-only. Hold-and-inspect surface for staged two-team War Room trades — fresh compliance, each owner's consent and conditional-drop selections, cap-acknowledgment status, and the real execution-ledger state — plus a confirmed Execute action for a trade that's ready.

⚠️ Required drops confirm FIRST, one at a time, before the trade is ever attempted (Keith's ruling, 2026-09-29; docs/LOADED_CONTRACT_DROP_EXECUTION_DESIGN.md §2.2/§2.4.3b) — never the reverse, and never left to per-deal judgment. This guarantees a franchise's roster never exceeds the 5-loaded-contract limit because of a deal this app handles. It does not make the trade safe: if every required drop confirms and the trade itself then fails, that is a real, largely irreversible loss — restoration is a manual, race-prone workaround (get the player back before anyone else claims him, then restore his exact contract), never a guarantee.

Clicking Execute re-derives compliance fresh at that exact moment and fails closed if anything has changed since this page loaded — the numbers shown below can go stale the instant something else moves a roster. Read them as "as of the last refresh," not as a live guarantee.
