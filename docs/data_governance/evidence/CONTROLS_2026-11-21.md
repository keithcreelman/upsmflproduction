## Top 10 missing controls, ranked by owner impact

1. **J3 is effectively blind because it is always red.** The fix is BBID_WAIVER classification plus a drop-rate budget. Real silent roster removals (the 2026-08-06 LOAD_ROSTERS class) and contract regressions (the 08-02 flatten of 18 contracts) now sit inside a 57% failure rate. Example: the Daiyan Henley false positive on 11-21 (V).
2. **MFL ingestion has no envelope or row-count validation** (GitHub snapshot, R2 snapshot, `rosters?.franchise || []`). Example: injuries stored empty for 111 days on green runs (V). A partial R2 day is never retried and it feeds drop-penalty pricing (V/I).
3. **No MFL ↔ D1 contract and cap reconciliation (the real J4)**, and the full `audit_contract_info.py` is not nightly. Front Office reads D1 mirrors. A contract wrong from the moment it is written, such as the 07-27 contract-year rollback (JSN/Jeudy/Roquan, internally self-consistent), passes both J3 and J4. No check compares UPS cap-used with MFL's ΣROSTER salaries + salaryAdj (`cap_parity` is code-only).
4. **Worker per-job failures are console-only.** The transactions ledger threw on every tick unnoticed; unpriced or held drops and stuck claims are logged, not DMed. The only heartbeat is scheduler-wide, and the Mac watchdog dies when the Mac sleeps.
5. **Salary-write audit coverage is 3 of about 11 paths, and ERA finalize fails open.** An unreadable salaries read becomes `{}`, which bypasses the never-flatten guard with no audit row. Example: `index.js:4241-4298` (per agent).
6. **No D1 backup or restore drill.** It relies on ad-hoc Time Travel bookmarks. The local SQLite backup is not installed. `BACKUP_STRATEGY.md` overstates coverage.
7. **No post-merge or scheduled run of the safe suite on main.** Main is red locally (13 fails on bc607887 per #1399), and the only detector is a bot-cutover issue with 158 comments.
8. **Freshness and source stamps are missing where owners decide.** `/api/standings`, `/api/franchise-assets`, `/api/player-status` and `/api/mfl-export` have none. Desktop-only D1 tables (`player_id_crosswalk`, `metric_stickiness`, `src_weekly`) carry no staleness signal. The acquisition hub reports green hourly while skipping (V).
9. **Discord ↔ ledger reconciliation is not running.** `discord-reconcile` is disabled; restructure and drops reconcilers were last run in May and June. Announcements that disagree with MFL or D1 go unnoticed.
10. **No completeness or coverage gate for historical and AI-facing D1 data.** `src_weekly` playoff weeks are missing or rostered-only (#1307, #1271, #1308), and the 17 gaps are tracked in #1386 and #1486. Apart from the one-off #1372 rank hold and the leaderboard coverage check, readers (Stats, AI/intelligence) get no flag. No scheduled D1 usage or budget alert exists either (`d1_usage.py` is manual), and GAP-01 is unmerged.
