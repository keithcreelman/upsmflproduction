+### Cap math invariants — locked, do not re-derive
+
+- **TCV is FIXED at the signed total contract value for the contract's life.** It does NOT decrement at rollovers. The cap penalty formula `(TCV × 75%) − Earned` uses the **original signed TCV** (see §6.D1 worked example: 3yr / $30K/yr contract has TCV $90K used in the formula even after Y1 is earned).
+- **Cap penalty formula is `(TCV × 75%) − Earned`** — NOT `(TCV − Earned) × 75%`. Example: 3yr $9K MYM (TCV $27K), cut after Y2 earned ($18K): cap hit = ($27K × 75%) − $18K = $20,250 − $18K = **$2,250**. The "75% of unearned" framing gives $6,750 and is WRONG.
+- **Earned uses the year's actual salary, not AAV** (line 420). For a Front-Loaded [62/44/17] contract: after Y1 rollover, Earned = $62K (not $41K AAV).
+- **Earned grows year-over-year as full years complete** at rollover. Mid-cycle events get prorated only per the era in effect.
+- **Season Rollover is NOT a contract event** — TCV/AAV/CL stay the same; only `yrs left` decrements and the year-just-completed's actual salary gets added to Earned.
+- **A new contract event** (FA Auction, Multi-Yr submission, Tag, Extension, MYM, Restructure) creates a NEW (TCV, AAV, CL, Earned=$0) tuple. Prior contract is closed.
+- **Contract completion** (yrs left → 0 via final rollover) means no further cap exposure on that contract — Earned will equal TCV. A subsequent "cut" of an already-expired contract is a $0 cap event.
+- **Build contract state from event chain, not rosters.** AUCTION_WON → MYAC/MYM/Ext/Restructure → trade → cut. The MFL rosters API can display post-event state retroactively across historical weeks, so it's not authoritative for game-time contract value. The Rodgers 2023 case is canonical: Mannila won at $50K, played 2023 NFL season as 1yr $50K Veteran, cut 12/21/2023 at $50K. Cap hits depend on game-time state, not the retroactively-displayed roster state.
+
+- **A cut TERMINATES the contract; the next acquisition is a NEW contract — never the same contract continuing.** Don't conflate distinct contracts as one transforming. The Rodgers 2023 case has TWO separate contracts:
+  - **Contract A (Mannila):** 1yr $50K Veteran. Lifecycle: auction 7/31/2023 → cut 12/21/2023 (terminates). Cap hit on cut: $0 (fully earned). Done.

+  - **Contract A and Contract B are separate.** They share a player but not a contract identity. Earned (cycle-to-date) resets at the start of each new contract. Stint attribution lives within a contract, not across.

+- **MYM/Ext/Restructure MODIFY a contract in-place** (extending length or adjusting salary distribution); they don't create a new contract. WW BBID, FA Auction, Tag, Dispersal, Trade-acquisition all involve EITHER a new contract starting (BBID/FA/Tag/Dispersal) or an existing contract carrying with new ownership (Trade).

+### Common fuckups to avoid
+
+- ❌ "TCV decreases at each rollover" — NO. TCV is fixed.
+- ❌ "Earned = AAV × years completed" — NO. Earned uses each year's ACTUAL salary (matters for FL/BL).
+- ❌ "Cut on an expired contract = cap penalty" — NO. yrs left = 0 means contract is already complete; subsequent cut is $0. (D1 currently has Pass 2 phantoms here — known bug.)
+- ❌ "Tag/Extension/MYM use the same TCV as the prior contract" — NO. Each is a NEW contract event with its own TCV/AAV/CL/Earned reset.
+- ❌ "All offseason cuts are real cap events" — NO. Some are **housekeeping cleanups**: when an owner manually drops a player whose contract had already expired at the prior March rollover. Pre-rollover-automation era, Keith handled rollovers manually for everyone; after the rollover-automation transition, owners clean up their own expired roster spots via manual drops. These are $0 cap events. Distinguish by checking `yrs left` at the moment of the cut: if `yrs left = 0` (contract already expired via prior rollover), the cut is housekeeping and cap-free regardless of MFL `move_type=DROP` flagging. (Pass 2 currently misclassifies these as "cuts" — the source of the 2021 Blake $27K and 2023 Mannila $58.5K phantoms in `player_acquisition_cycles`.)
+- ❌ "WW BBID pickup carries the previous owner's contract" — NO. **Only TRADE (A6 line 266) and DISPERSAL (A7 line 279) carry contracts forward.** WW BBID and FCFS pickups create a **NEW 1-year WW contract** at the BBID/$1K amount (per A4 line 244-245). FA Auction wins also create a NEW contract. After a cut + WW rebid of the same player, the prior multi-year contract is terminated; the new BBID is a fresh 1-year deal. Owner has the post-pickup window (14 days MYM, 15-28 days extension per C4) to convert to multi-year — NOT a transparent carryover of prior terms.
+- ❌ "MFL `TYPE=rosters` salary is the contract value at signing" — NO. **The auction-winning bid (MFL `TYPE=transactions` `AUCTION_WON` `desc=pid|amount|`) is the source of truth for Y1 contract value.** The rosters/salary API can be out of sync with the actual contract value due to admin overrides, restructures not propagated, or MFL data-staleness. Always cross-reference the `AUCTION_WON` event amount against the rosters salary; if they disagree, **trust the auction price** for the contract value at signing. The salary-adjustment record at cut time (`TYPE=salaryAdjustments` describing the dropped contract) is also more authoritative than rosters for the contract state at the moment of the drop. This lesson came from Rodgers 2023: `AUCTION_WON desc=7836|50000|` and the 12/21/2023 cut record showed `Salary: $50,000, CL: 1, Type: Veteran` — but rosters W=1 through W=15 showed `$9K cy=3 CL=3`. Pass 2 used rosters → wrong cap hit. Fix: use auction-winning bid + cut salary-adjustment record as the source of truth.

+### Per-owner Earned attribution
+
+When a contract is held by multiple owners across its life (via trade, dispersal, WW pickup carryover), the cumulative cycle-to-date Earned must be **decomposable per owner stint**. A "stint" is a continuous-ownership period under one contract. Stint boundaries:
+- **Stint starts:** new contract signing (FA win), trade-in, dispersal-in, WW BBID pickup carrying contract, tag application.
+- **Stint ends:** trade-out, real cut (with cap impact), housekeeping cut, contract completion (final rollover yrs left → 0).
+
+For each event row in a contract timeline, surface BOTH:
+- **Earned (cycle-to-date)** — running cumulative across all owners on this contract.
+- **Earned (this owner's stint)** — resets to $0 at the current owner's stint start, grows with each rollover/in-season event during their tenure.
+
+Attribution at rollover: the year's actual salary is credited toward whichever owner held the player for the bulk of that NFL season's weeks (or split proportionally per the era rule, if mid-year ownership change). For trade-out events, the outgoing owner's stint Earned = the cycle-to-date right before the trade. The incoming owner's stint Earned begins at $0.
+
+Worked example — 2yr Veteran $18K flat, Cutting acquired via auction 8/19/2019, traded to Blake 11/24/2020 (mid-Y2 W=11→12), housekeeping-cut by Blake 6/1/2021:
+
+| Date | Event | Owner | Earned (cycle, cal-monthly) | Earned (this owner's stint) |
+|---|---|---|---|---|
+| 8/19/2019 | FA Auction | Cutting | $0 | $0 (Cutting stint starts) |
+| 3/1/2020 | Rollover (Y1=$18K earned) | Cutting | $18,000 | $18,000 |
+| 11/24/2020 | Trade (3/4 × Y2 = $13.5K partial) | (Cutting → Blake) | $31,500 | Cutting stint ends: **$31,500** \| Blake stint starts: $0 |
+| 3/1/2021 | Rollover (Y2 complete; remainder credited to Blake) | Blake | $36,000 | $4,500 (Blake stint to date) |
+| 6/1/2021 | Housekeeping cut (yrs left = 0) | Blake | $36,000 | $4,500 (stint stayed; $0 cap impact) |
+
+Cutting's total stint earnings: $31,500. Blake's total stint earnings: $4,500. Sum = $36,000 = TCV (contract fully earned). The $0 cap hit on the 6/1/2021 cut reflects the housekeeping nature.

+### C10. MFL `TYPE=rosters` reliability caveats — pre-2020 work especially (added 2026-05-10)
+
+`TYPE=rosters&L={league_id}&W={N}` does NOT return a clean point-in-time snapshot for pre-2020 seasons. Two distinct behaviors that need to be kept separate when reading older roster files:
+
+- **Roster composition (which players are on which franchise) IS per-week-accurate.** Pulling W=01 vs W=07 vs W=14 for a pre-2020 season returns genuinely different player lists per franchise, reflecting actual mid-season acquisitions, drops, and trades up to that week. Empirical confirmation: 2012 W=01..W=17 files differ in size and per-franchise player counts (e.g., f=0010 = 26 players at W=01, 28 at W=14).
+
+- **Per-player contract fields (salary, contractYear, contractInfo) are END-OF-SEASON-stamped, NOT at-pickup or per-week.** A WW pickup acquired in Week 9 will appear on the post-pickup franchise's W=01 roster (composition is correct for "where they ended up") but the `salary` shown will be the EOS salary, not the at-pickup salary that the AUCTION_WON / BBID_WAIVER transaction recorded. Same for any contract that gets MYM'd / extended / restructured later in the season — the modified salary/cy reflects retroactively across all W=N files for that season.
+
+**Verification protocol** when reading any pre-2020 roster file: cross-check a known mid-season pickup's salary against the `transaction` field on the corresponding `BBID_WAIVER` / `FREE_AGENT` event. If they disagree, the roster is showing the EOS-stamped salary — trust the transaction.
+
+**Operating rule for cap-math derivation:**
+1. **Never use rosters W=N as primary source for contract value at a moment in time.**
+2. Build state from the **event chain** — `AUCTION_WON desc=pid|amount|` for Y1; UPS-side `mym_submissions` / `extension_submissions` / `restructure_submissions` for in-place modifications; `TYPE=trades` for ownership changes; `TYPE=salaryAdjustments` salary description for state-at-cut.
+3. Use rosters only for (a) composition / who's-on-which-franchise, (b) `contractInfo` text annotations (apostrophe-year extension hints, "Franchised by X" tag notes), (c) cross-validation of derived state at the END of the season (where EOS-stamped values ARE accurate by definition).
+
+The Rodgers 2023 case ([§ "Common fuckups", line 111](#common-fuckups-to-avoid)) is the canonical worked example for the modern era; the 2011 EOS rollover anomaly ([§4.D, line 1601](#d-source-data-conflicts-to-resolve)) is the canonical pre-2020 worked example. Both are instances of this same underlying API behavior.
